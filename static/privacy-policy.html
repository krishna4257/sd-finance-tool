Privacy Policy for Smart Counting

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Smart Counting mobile application ("App") provided by Siva Durga Finance ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how we handle sensitive user and operational information.

1. Scope of the Application

Smart Counting is an internal enterprise tool designed strictly for authorized staff and employees to manage customer ledgers, daily collections, loan tracking, and field accounting records.

2. Information We Collect

  • User Authentication: Staff login credentials, user identifiers, and authorized device IDs to verify permissions.
  • Operational & Transaction Data: Customer payment amounts, installment records, and ledger entries recorded by authorized personnel.
  • Location Information: With staff permission, foreground/background location is used solely to record field collection routes, verify collection points, and optimize route navigation.
  • Camera & Files: With your permission, the camera and storage are accessed solely to capture and upload customer identification documents or receipt records into the secure accounting system.

3. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate staff and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To record, process, and reconcile daily financial collections and ledger balances.
  • To verify field collection operations and maintain accurate audit logs.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or operational data to third parties. Data is never shared with third-party advertising networks. Data is strictly transmitted to our secure internal servers for business ledger processing.

5. Data Security

We employ enterprise-grade security practices, including TLS/HTTPS encrypted network communications, restricted API authentication, and device-level security (including screenshot prevention flags) to safeguard sensitive operational records.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained in accordance with financial regulatory and ledger compliance guidelines. Authorized users can contact company administration to request account review or data rectification.
