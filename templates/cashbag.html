Cash Bag Formula & Accounting Reconciliation

Live comparison: Physical Cash In Bag vs. Customer Receivables (Dashboard)

Formula 1 💵 Physical Cash in Hand (Cash Bag Total)
₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(cashbag_total|float) }} Cash: ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(cashbag_total_cash|float) }} | UPI: ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(cashbag_total_upi|float) }}
Cash Bag Total = Capital Deployed + Collection + Rusum + Commission - Loans Dispensed
{% set net_misc = (office_cash|float) + (adjustments_add|float) + (profit_loss_net|float) - (capital_withdrawn|default(0)|float) - (expenses|float) - (adjustments_minus|float) %} {% if net_misc != 0 %} {% endif %}
Capital Deployed +₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(capital_deployed|default(0)|float) }}
Total Collection (Customer Repayments) +₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(installments|float) }}
Rusum (Cutting collected upfront) +₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(loan_commission|float) }}
Commission (Interest collected upfront) +₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_total|float) }}
Loans Dispensed (Gross Loan Amount) -₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(loans_disbursed|float) }}
Net Other (Office Cash, Expenses, Adjustments, P&L) {{ '+' if net_misc >= 0 else '' }}₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(net_misc|float) }}
= Physical Cash in Bag ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(cashbag_total|float) }}
Net Disbursed Cash Equivalence:
Gross Loans (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(loans_disbursed|float) }}) minus Rusum (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(loan_commission|float) }}) minus Commission (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_total|float) }}) equals ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(base_amount_total|default(0)|float) }} (Actual Base Cash handed to borrowers).
Capital (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(capital_deployed|default(0)|float) }}) + Collections (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(installments|float) }}) - Base Cash Disbursed (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(base_amount_total|default(0)|float) }}) = ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(cashbag_total|float) }}
Why Interest Discount (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_discount_total|default(0)|float) }}) is NOT Deducted Here:
Interest Discount is a non-cash settlement concession. When you grant a discount to close a borrower's account, zero cash leaves the cash bag. Deducting ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_discount_total|default(0)|float) }} from this bag total would falsely report ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format((cashbag_total - (interest_discount_total|default(0)))|float) }}, which would cause a fake ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_discount_total|default(0)|float) }} cash shortage error against your physical rupee notes!
Formula 2 📊 Customer Receivables (Village Dashboard View)
₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(outstanding_balance_total|default(0)|float) }} Active: ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(active_balance_total|default(0)|float) }} | Defaulted: ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(((outstanding_balance_total|default(0)) - (active_balance_total|default(0)))|float) }}
Outstanding Balance = Gross Loans Dispensed - Collections - Interest Discount Waived
Total Gross Loans Dispensed ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(loans_disbursed|float) }}
Less: Total Cash Collections -₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(installments|float) }}
Less: Interest Discount Waived (Settlement) -₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_discount_total|default(0)|float) }}
= Total Outstanding Balance Remaining ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(outstanding_balance_total|default(0)|float) }}
This is where Interest Discount takes effect!
Because the borrower paid their settlement cash and you waived ₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(interest_discount_total|default(0)|float) }} interest, their remaining debt drops to ₹0. The discount reduces what borrowers owe you on the Village Dashboard, rather than taking cash out of your pocket.
Dual-Ledger Mathematical Integrity: 100% Balanced
Physical Cash in Hand (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(cashbag_total|float) }}) + Borrower Receivables (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(outstanding_balance_total|default(0)|float) }}) = Tangible Assets (₹{{ "{:,.0f}".format(((cashbag_total|default(0)) + (outstanding_balance_total|default(0)))|float) }})
